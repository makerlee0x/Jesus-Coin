Deploy the JESUS bridge

Four steps. Each button asks your wallet to confirm a transaction. Do the whole thing on testnet first. Your progress is saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back.

1. Deploy the Ethereum adapter

The vault on . JESUS sent to Robinhood is locked here and released when it comes back. On testnet a test JESUS token (777.8T supply, sent to you) is created first.

3. Connect the two sides

Tells each side the other's address and switches traffic on (no daily limit). Up to three confirmations per side; steps already done are skipped.

Security: LayerZero Labs verifier (DVN) on both directions. Two confirmations per side.

4. Test a transfer

Send a small amount to Robinhood. Delivery takes a few minutes; follow it on LayerZero Scan. Then send it back.
